# Privacy

ModHub has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer receives no data.

- Your searches and download requests go directly from your phone to MCPEDL, CurseForge and (if enabled) Modrinth.
- Your CurseForge API key, if you enter one, is stored only on your phone and sent only to CurseForge.
- Downloads are saved to the app's private cache and handed to Minecraft.
- **Display over other apps**: used only to draw one invisible 1-pixel dot so ModHub can open Settings from the background during the optional restart.
- **Accessibility service**: declared for `com.android.settings` only; it taps "Force stop" and "OK" on Minecraft's App info page and does not read or store anything.
